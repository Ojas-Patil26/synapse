# Synapse UI copy

Exact strings. Use them verbatim.

## Landing page (public)

**Top right buttons:** `Log in` · `Sign up`

**Headline:** See what you know. See what's slipping.

**Subhead:** Synapse turns your course notes into a living map of every concept,
colored by how well you still remember it and outlined by how well you understood it.
It predicts what you'll forget next, so you can review it before it's gone.

**Primary button:** `Sign up`
**Secondary button:** `Log in`

**Feature blocks**

1. **A map of every course** — Upload typed or handwritten notes. Synapse pulls out
   the concepts and how they depend on each other.
2. **Memory you can see** — Each concept fades from green to red along a forgetting
   curve fitted to your own review history.
3. **Remembering isn't understanding** — Short quizzes measure how well you grasp a
   concept, separately from how recently you saw it.
4. **Courses that connect** — When two courses share an idea, Synapse links them, so
   what you learn in one strengthens the other.

**Closing line:** Stop rereading everything. Review what's about to fade.

## Upload Course

**Page title (no courses yet):** Upload your first course
**Page title (has courses):** Upload a course

**Intro (no courses yet):** Add your notes and Synapse will build the concept map.
Typed or handwritten, PDF or photo.

**Field label:** Course name
**Field placeholder:** e.g. CSE 360

**Drop zone, main line:** Click to upload or drag files here
**Drop zone, second line:** PDF, PNG, JPG, TXT or MD · up to 5 files, 10 MB each
**Drop zone button:** `Choose File`

**Submit button:** `Build my map`

**Processing:** Building your map. Reading your notes and finding the concepts can
take a minute or two.

**Failed:** We couldn't build a map from those files. Check that the notes are
readable, then try again.

**Validation, no name:** Enter a course name.
**Validation, no file:** Add at least one file.
**Validation, wrong type:** That file type isn't supported. Use PDF, PNG, JPG, TXT or MD.
**Validation, too large:** That file is over 10 MB.
