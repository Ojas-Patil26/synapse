## Mode

Persuade (landing). Operate surfaces inherit the same tokens.

## World

Startup stage on **Jet Black** `#0A0A0A`, with **Cerulean** `#007BA7` as the interactive accent and **Neon Rose** `#FF2D95` as the signal/energy colour. Geist Sans. Backdrop: two abstracted arms reaching toward a synapse spark (connection metaphor), plus soft brand washes — not flat void, not purple aurora.

Retention on the concept graph stays a separate red → amber → green ramp (product meaning), not brand chrome.

## Taste dials

- Layout variance: **6**
- Motion: **4** (entry reveals + one spark pulse on the reach art)
- Density: **3**

## Tokens

| Role | Token | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Stage | `--page` | `#0A0A0A` (dark) / cool paper (light) |
